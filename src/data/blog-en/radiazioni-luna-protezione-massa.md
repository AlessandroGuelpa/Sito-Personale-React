Earth's atmosphere and magnetic field help shield the surface from the space environment. Beyond low Earth orbit, a crew encounters a different environment. Protecting that crew affects the design of the vehicle, its layout and how much mass it needs to carry.

[Potential lunar ice use](/blog/ghiaccio-lunare-risorsa-utilizzabile) raises a related question about which resources might be available locally. [Orion's thermal control](/blog/orion-controllo-termico-radiatori-vuoto) deals with another form of energy that the vehicle must manage.

## Identify the exposure the mission needs to handle

Solar particle events and galactic cosmic rays have different characteristics and vary on different timescales. A mission's duration and trajectory matter to the assessment, along with the radiation environment and the geometry of the vehicle. A useful shielding requirement specifies which exposure it addresses and under what operating conditions. Material thickness alone tells only part of that story.

## Give existing mass a second job

NASA describes ways Orion can use materials already on board to increase shielding around an area where the crew can shelter during a solar particle event. Using available supplies or equipment for more than one purpose can affect how much dedicated shielding the spacecraft must carry. Placement still matters because the shelter has to work within the layout and remain accessible to the crew.

Every kilogram also affects other engineering choices. Design the shelter alongside the structure, propulsion and placement of other systems so its mass and volume are accounted for in the rest of the mission.

## Ask how protection is assessed

When I read a lunar habitat proposal, I look for the link between the expected environment and the protection that was evaluated. What exposure is being considered, for how long, and under which conditions? The answer requires mission-specific radiation and dose modelling. A drawing of a thick wall alone gives too little information to judge the protection that wall can provide.
