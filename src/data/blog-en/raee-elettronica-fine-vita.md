An old phone in a drawer has stopped serving a purpose without entering a recovery system. It may sit next to a charger and another device whose fault nobody remembers. Electronics bring together valuable materials, complicated components and personal information. Each affects what should happen to the device next.

Start with [repairability and the design of products](/blog/riparabilita-progetto-prodotti). If a device cannot usefully be repaired, [the quality of collected materials](/blog/riciclo-raccolta-qualita-materiale) matters to what can be recovered after disposal.

## Check whether the device can still do a job

A working device may be suitable for reuse or refurbishment. Consider its condition, software support and whether someone actually needs it. When equipment has reached the end of its useful life, a collection route for waste electrical and electronic equipment, or WEEE, enables appropriate treatment.

The European Commission identifies recoverable resources in this material and substances that need careful handling. Getting a device into an appropriate collection system gives treatment facilities a chance to separate the parts and materials they can use.

## Different routes preserve different things

Keeping a working product in use preserves the function it performs. Material recovery starts with separating and processing its components. A device containing recyclable materials can still be difficult to repair or short-lived. Manufacturing a replacement also requires resources, so useful product life and material recovery are both worth considering.

## Finish the data journey as well

Before handing over a device, check backups, unlink accounts and follow the manufacturer's erasure process for that model. The right sequence can depend on storage, encryption and whether an organisation manages the device. For disposal, use the official local collection route and check its device-specific instructions before taking anything apart.

Sorting a drawer into devices still useful, repairable or ready for collection gives each item a next step. Storage by default delays that decision while the opportunity for useful reuse can shrink.
